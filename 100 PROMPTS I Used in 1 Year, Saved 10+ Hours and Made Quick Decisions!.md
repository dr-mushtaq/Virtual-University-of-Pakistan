## How to Use This Library

1. Choose a prompt that matches the task you need help with.
2. Replace every placeholder in `[square brackets]` with your own information.
3. Include useful context and be as specific as possible.
4. Copy the completed prompt into ChatGPT.

### Three Useful Instructions to Add to Any Prompt

You can add any of these lines to the end of a prompt:

- `Before you start, ask me up to 3 questions.`
- `Give me 3 versions so I can pick.`
- `If you're unsure about anything, mark it [CHECK].`

## 1. Emails and texts

For the messages you keep putting off.

### 1. Say no politely

> Write a polite reply saying no to [request] from [person]. Keep the relationship warm. Offer [alternative] instead. Under 80 words.

### 6. Thank-you note

### 7. Ask for a favor

### 8. Group chat announcement

### 9. Apology

### 10. Cut it in half

## What ChatGPT Still Can't Do Automatically

- It may not have live prices, opening hours, or flight status unless you provide the information or use an available search feature. Double-check time-sensitive details.
- It does not automatically know your family, budget, preferences, or circumstances. Add those details to the prompts.
- **Never paste sensitive information** such as passwords, bank logins, or government identification numbers into a prompt.
